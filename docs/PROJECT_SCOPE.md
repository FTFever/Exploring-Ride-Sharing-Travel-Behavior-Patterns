# Research scope

This repository exhibits the current pickup-demand components of a broader study of ridesharing travel behavior and forecasting in New York City. Descriptive analyses use 2021–2025. Hourly zonal forecasts use 2021–2025 training with a January 1–7, 2026 final test. Current results are observational. Planned work extends to dropoffs, origin–destination pairs, training-history experiments and causal impacts of congestion pricing.
